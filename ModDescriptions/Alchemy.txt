ALCHEMY - PROPOSAL  (for approval; nothing below is built)

A mod of potions: things gathered, ground, brewed and bottled, with something worth drinking, throwing or
pouring out at every stage of the game - from a poultice made at a campfire on the first night to an elixir
that takes the whole of the late game to brew.

The game already has a mod called Alchemie: benches that turn one material into another, six essences, the
philosopher's stone and the Transmutation Table. It has no potions at all. See question 1.

Names and numbers below are proposals. Anything can be renamed, cut or swapped.

================================================================================================================
0. QUESTIONS
================================================================================================================

Q1. ONE MOD OR TWO?
    A (recommended). Put all of this INTO Alchemie. It becomes one mod with two halves - transmutation (what it
       has) and potions (this) - sharing the alembic and the six essences. One thing to switch on, and the
       essences get a second use.
    B. A separate mod, "Apothecary". Cleaner to switch on and off, but it cannot use Alchemie's essences or
       benches (a mod may not depend on another), so it needs its own, and the two will overlap in theme.

Q2. EFFECTS THAT LAST. Nothing in the game lasts for a while and then stops: food fills a bar, clothes work
    while worn. A potion needs "for the next three minutes". That is new engine work and a new save version
    (each character's running effects are saved). I would build it so that food and anything else can use it
    too - Dimensions' drinks were meant to and could not. Yes?

Q3. HOW MANY AT ONCE? Recommended: a **toxicity** bar. Every potion adds to it; it drains with time; the fuller
    it is the weaker the next potion, and at the top the character is sick (slow, no effect from potions) until
    it falls. Stronger potions cost more of it. It makes "which three do I drink before this" a decision,
    and gives the mod a reason for antidotes and for clothing that raises the limit.
    The plain alternative: at most three effects at once, a fourth replaces the oldest.

Q4. THROWN POTIONS. Recommended yes: a flask thrown at a square (a click, in reach of a bow) bursts and does
    something to what is there - creatures, blocks, water, plants. This is where the world-altering ones live.

Q5. WHERE INGREDIENTS COME FROM. New plants in the game's own biomes only appear in worlds made after the mod
    is on. Recommended: every herb also has a recipe or a drop that an old world can reach (seeds sold at the
    trade portal, or grown from something the world already has), so an existing save is not locked out.

Q6. THE LATE GAME. Dimensions adds tiers VII to X. A mod cannot depend on another, but a loot pool or a biome
    list may name things an absent mod has and simply skip them. Recommended: four top-tier potions whose
    ingredients are Aether and Deep things, which only exist - and only appear in the recipe book - when
    Dimensions is on too. Everything else is complete without it.

Q7. SIZE. As proposed: about 45 blocks, 150 items, 190 recipes, 9 creature drops put to use, one new save
    version. Say if it should be bigger or smaller.

================================================================================================================
1. HOW IT WORKS
================================================================================================================

A potion is three things: a BASE (what it is carried in), a REAGENT (what it does) and often a CATALYST (how
strongly, or for how long).

  Bases       water (weak, short), spirit of wine (the standard), oil (for things rubbed on, not drunk),
              quicksilver (the strongest; raises toxicity most).
  Reagents    herbs, fungi, creature parts, minerals, essences.
  Catalysts   salt (longer), sugar (kinder: less toxicity), ember (stronger), gem dust (stronger and longer:
              amethyst, then sapphire and so on up the tiers - each gem's dust is the catalyst of its tier).

Every drinkable potion comes in three strengths - plain, strong (II) and grand (III) - made at successive
tiers of the cauldron. So one reagent gives a potion that is still worth making three tiers later.

Kinds of potion:
  DRAUGHT     drunk; an effect for a time.
  TONIC       drunk; something at once (health, hunger, a cure) and nothing lasting.
  OIL         rubbed on the tool or weapon in hand; it lasts so many uses.
  FLASK       thrown; bursts where it lands.
  SALVE       put on the body's worn things; lasts while they are worn, wears off with time.
  ELIXIR      the great works: one each, hard to make, with an effect that does not wear off.

Bottles are glass, made at the kiln, and come back empty when a potion is drunk.

================================================================================================================
2. INGREDIENTS
================================================================================================================

Herbs (15, each a crop that can be sown, with a biome it grows wild in):
  feverfew (plains)        bloodroot (forest)       marshmallow (shore)      nightshade (anywhere, by night)
  sunwort (desert)         frostcap (cold places)   kingsfoil (mountains)    mandrake (rare, deep soil)
  dreamleaf (orchard)      glowmoss (caves)         firethorn (desert)       willowbark (from any willow or oak)
  sea lavender (shore)     wolfsbane (pine forest)  ghost orchid (cave lakes, rare)

Fungi (5, cave floors): inkcap, puffball, stinkhorn, brain fungus, death cap.

From creatures the game has: dodo feather and egg, dropbear claw (new drop), scorpion venom (it has), shark
fin (new drop), fish oil (from fish), yak milk and horn (it has), and one new thing from each gem dodo.

Minerals: salt, sulphur (new vein), saltpetre (cave walls), gem dusts (any gem at the mortar), coal, quicksilver.

Made along the way: spirit of wine (fermented from wheat or fruit), vinegar, lye, tinctures (a herb steeped in
spirit - keeps for ever, and is what most recipes actually take), extracts (a tincture distilled: what the
strong potions take), and the essences Alchemie already has.

================================================================================================================
3. BENCHES
================================================================================================================

  Mortar and pestle     workbench I.   Grinds: powders, pastes, gem dust.
  Drying rack           workbench I.   Dries herbs (they keep, and weigh less in a recipe).
  Cauldron I            workbench I.   Over a fire. Water-based potions: teas, poultices, weak draughts.
  Fermenting crock      workbench II.  Spirit of wine, vinegar, the things that take days.
  Cauldron II (copper)  upgrade.       Spirit-based potions: the standard draughts.
  Alembic               (Alchemie's, or this mod's own if separate.) Tinctures into extracts; oils.
  Cauldron III (iron)   upgrade.       Strong (II) potions; flasks.
  Infuser               workbench III. Oils and salves.
  Cauldron IV (gold)    upgrade.       Grand (III) potions.
  Athanor               (Alchemie's.)  Elixirs.
  Apothecary's cabinet  workbench II.  A chest of 32 small slots that only takes potions and ingredients.
  Potion belt           tailor.        Worn; four potions on it can be drunk with a key each, without the bar.

================================================================================================================
4. THE POTIONS, BY STAGE OF THE GAME
================================================================================================================

STAGE 1 - FIRST DAYS (flint and stone; a campfire; cauldron I). Teas and poultices, from one herb and water.
  Poultice            tonic     mends a quarter of health over ten seconds.
  Willowbark tea      draught   health mends twice as fast for 3 minutes.
  Feverfew tea        draught   the cold gets to you half as fast (the environment score) for 5 minutes.
  Dodo broth          tonic     fills hunger; the first "cooked" food that needs no bench but the cauldron.
  Wakeful tea         draught   energy does not run down for 3 minutes.
  Bitter draught      tonic     cures poison.
  Torchwater          flask     a square it lands on glows for a day, as a torch would, with nothing to hold it.

STAGE 2 - COPPER AND TIN (cauldron II; the crock). The standard draughts.
  Swiftness           draught   walks a quarter faster.
  Miner's draught     draught   digs a quarter faster.
  Stoneskin           draught   a fifth of every blow turned.
  Gills               draught   breath lasts four times as long.
  Owl sight           draught   it is never darker than dusk to you (a floor under the light you see by).
  Stillness           draught   hunger runs down half as fast.
  Growth tonic        flask     every plant within 2 squares grows a stage.
  Weed-killer         flask     every plant within 2 squares is taken up and left lying.
  Snare               flask     creatures within 2 squares cannot move for 10 seconds.

STAGE 3 - IRON (cauldron III; the alembic; flasks). Strong potions, and potions that act on the world.
  Strong (II) forms of every stage 2 draught.
  Strength            draught   every blow does more.
  Thorns              draught   whatever bites you is hurt.
  Feather fall        draught   a drop of any height is a step (what a glider does).
  Craftsman's draught draught   work at a bench goes a third faster.
  Fortune             draught   a block dug sometimes leaves one more.
  Frost flask         flask     water within 2 squares freezes; creatures are slowed.
  Fire flask          flask     everything that burns within 2 squares burns.
  Acid flask          flask     plain blocks within 1 square dissolve and leave nothing.
  Whetstone oil       oil       the tool in hand works half again as fast for 200 blocks.
  Venom oil           oil       the weapon in hand poisons what it hits, for 40 blows.

STAGE 4 - STEEL AND GOLD (cauldron IV; the infuser). Grand potions; salves; the first strange ones.
  Grand (III) forms of the stage 2 draughts; strong forms of stage 3's.
  Invisibility        draught   creatures do not see you unless you hit one.
  Magnetism           draught   things lying within 6 squares come to you.
  Second wind         draught   the first time health would run out, it is set to a third instead. Once.
  Recall              tonic     takes you to the portal you last used, at no cost in crystals.
  Warming salve       salve     on clothes: they keep half again as much of the cold out, for a day.
  Cooling salve       salve     the same against heat (for Dimensions' hot places, and useless without them).
  Rain bottle         flask     thrown into the sky: it rains for a day.
  Sun bottle          flask     thrown into the sky: it stops raining, and the next rain is put off a day.
  Gem-seed tonic      flask     thrown at a gem set in a cave wall: another grows beside it.

STAGE 5 - TITANIUM AND GEMS (the athanor). Elixirs, and the potions that change what a world is.
  Grand forms of everything in stages 3 and 4.
  Flask of the tide   flask     where it lands becomes a spring: water rises there for a minute.
  Flask of drought    flask     all water within 6 squares is gone.
  Stoneshaper         flask     plain blocks within 3 squares become the block it is thrown AT (a wall of
                                dirt struck where it meets marble becomes marble).
  Midas oil           oil       the pickaxe in hand makes ore leave half again as much, for 100 blocks.
  ELIXIR OF VIGOUR              health bar a quarter longer, for good.
  ELIXIR OF THE DEEP BREATH     breath twice as long, for good.
  ELIXIR OF THE LONG ROAD       a tenth quicker on foot, for good.
  ELIXIR OF IRON STOMACH        toxicity drains twice as fast, for good.
  PANACEA                       cures everything, empties toxicity, fills every bar. One use.
  (An elixir can be drunk once by each character. Each needs a philosopher's stone's worth of work, not a
  stone: its recipe is a tree eight to ten makings deep.)

STAGE 6 - WITH DIMENSIONS ON (see Q6). Four potions of Aether and Deep ingredients.
  Skywalker's draught           walk on air: every square is ground to you for 30 seconds.
  Draught of the abyss          magma does not burn you for 3 minutes.
  Stormcaller                   flask: lightning strikes where it lands (a blast of radius 2, and fire).
  ELIXIR OF THE SECOND SHADOW   a potion's effect lasts twice as long on you, for good.

That is 58 kinds of potion, about 95 counting the strengths.

================================================================================================================
5. THINGS BESIDES POTIONS
================================================================================================================

  Herb garden beds       a block that herbs grow twice as fast in.
  Hanging herbs, bottles on a shelf, a bubbling cauldron: things to furnish a workroom with.
  The Herbal             a book (as the Tome of Knowledge is): carried, the recipe book shows what every potion
                         does and for how long, which it otherwise only says once you have drunk one.
  Antidote and the sick  creatures that poison (scorpions) get a second kind of venom at night; the Bitter
                         draught cures one and a new Antivenom the other.
  Trade                  potions sell well and are light: with Rags to Riches on, a thing to carry between
                         villages. (No dependence: they simply have prices.)
  A mob                  the Bog witch, in shore biomes by night: hostile, throws flasks, drops rare reagents
                         and now and then a potion you cannot yet make.

================================================================================================================
6. ENGINE WORK
================================================================================================================

All as data fields the base game could use; no "if Alchemy is on".

  Timed effects     An item, drunk or eaten, gives boons for so many ticks ("effect": {"boons": ..., "ticks": N}).
                    Kept per character and saved: one new save version, every older save still loading.
                    The game already adds up boons from armour and relics in one place (game_boons): effects
                    are a third thing added there.
  New boons         glide, see in the dark, unseen by creatures, draw things to you, immune to what burns,
                    walk on air, a floor under health once. Each a field beside the twelve there are.
  Toxicity          one more bar per character (Q3), with the rules above; shown with the other bars.
  Thrown things     an item that is thrown at a square in range and does one of: grow plants, clear plants,
                    freeze, burn, dissolve blocks, add or remove water, hold creatures, change the weather,
                    turn blocks into another. Most of these exist already as spells (Magic and Mayhem's wands
                    cast water, dig, and damage) - a flask is a spell used up in one throw.
  Oils and salves   a number kept with the single item in hand or worn (the "meter" tools and buckets have):
                    uses left, and which oil.
  Lasting changes   an elixir raises a limit for one character for good: saved with them.
  Benches           the cauldron tiers use "extends" (so they sit after anything another mod adds).
  The recipe book   says what a potion does; hides it until drunk unless the Herbal is carried.
  Tests             one for each rule; a fixture of the save version being left behind.

================================================================================================================
7. ORDER OF WORK, IF APPROVED
================================================================================================================

  1. Timed effects, toxicity, the save version; six stage-1 potions to prove them.
  2. Ingredients: herbs, fungi, drops, veins; the mortar, rack, crock and cauldrons; stages 2 and 3 draughts.
  3. Thrown flasks, oils and salves.
  4. Stage 4 and 5: the strange ones, the world-changing flasks, the elixirs and their trees.
  5. The belt, the cabinet, the Herbal, the witch, furniture; prices; the README.
  Each stage tested, documented, committed and pushed.
