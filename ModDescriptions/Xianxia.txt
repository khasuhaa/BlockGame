PATH OF IMMORTALS - PROPOSAL  (a xianxia mod; for approval - nothing below is built)

A character who meditates on a spirit vein, eats what grows there, takes the right pill at the right moment and
survives the lightning becomes, a realm at a time, something that is not a mortal any more. Fifty steps:

    QI REFINING I-X  ->  FOUNDATION BUILDING I-X  ->  CORE FORMATION I-X  ->  NASCENT SOUL I-X  ->  DIVINITY I-X

and at the end of the fiftieth, Immortality. Around that road: spirit herbs that are worth more the older they
are, a pill furnace, formations laid out in blocks on the ground, techniques learned from manuals, spirit beasts
to fight, tame and ride, spirit food, talismans, flying swords, secret realms, and the heavens' objection to all
of it.

It is meant to be three or four times the size of the base game (which has 356 blocks, 429 items, 505 recipes).
Names and numbers are proposals. Anything can be renamed, cut or swapped.

================================================================================================================
0. QUESTIONS
================================================================================================================

Q1. HOW QI IS GAINED. Recommended: mostly by MEDITATING (the game already has meditation), at a rate set by where
    you sit - the qi of the place - times your manual, your spirit roots, and any formation, pill or food at
    work. A little also comes from fighting spirit beasts and from eating spirit food. So the loop is: find or
    build a good place to sit, feed it, and go out for what the next breakthrough needs.
    Alternative: qi from doing things (digging, fighting, crafting) - no sitting, but cultivation is then just
    a second experience bar.

Q2. HOW STRONG A CULTIVATOR GETS. Each realm should feel like a different creature, but the base game must not
    become trivial at Qi Refining III. Recommended:
      - each STAGE: a little more health, qi, and speed at everything (2% or so);
      - each REALM: a step - Foundation Building halves hunger and doubles breath; Core Formation needs no food
        and flies on a sword; Nascent Soul survives one death a day; Divinity needs no sword;
      - and the mod brings its own dangers to match (spirit beasts, tribulations, secret realms), so the power is
        spent there rather than on dodos.
    Say if you would rather it were wilder (a Core Formation cultivator one-shots everything in the overworld).

Q3. DEATH. Recommended: dying costs qi (back to the start of the current stage), never a realm. A failed
    breakthrough or a failed tribulation costs more (see 3). Alternative: no penalty beyond the game's own.

Q4. SPIRIT ROOTS. Each character is born with roots in the five elements (metal, wood, water, fire, earth), set
    by chance when they are made: fewer roots cultivate faster but learn fewer techniques. Recommended: random,
    shown from the start, changeable late and at great cost (a Root-Washing Pill). Alternative: chosen at
    character creation.

Q5. LIFESPAN. The genre's clock: a mortal has 80 years, each realm adds centuries, and running out is death.
    Recommended: NOT in the first version. It punishes exactly the slow, careful play the rest rewards, and the
    game's years are short. It can be a world setting later.

Q6. TRIBULATIONS. At each new realm the heavens send lightning: a fight against the sky, in the open, that
    formations, talismans, pills and armour all help with. Recommended: yes, four of them (into Foundation, Core,
    Nascent Soul, Divinity) and a ninefold one for Immortality. They are the mod's bosses.

Q7. OTHER PEOPLE. A xianxia world has sects, elders, markets, rivals. This game has no people who talk.
    Recommended for the first version: places without people - ruined sect halls, sealed caves of dead
    cultivators, altars - which hold manuals and inheritances, and "rogue cultivators" as hostile creatures.
    Sects you can join, missions and contribution points would be a second mod's worth of work on top.

Q8. SECRET REALMS. The engine now has other places (the Aether and the Deep are made with it). Recommended:
    two here - the SPIRIT REALM (reached at Foundation Building; a wild land thick with qi, old herbs and
    beasts) and the IMMORTAL RUINS (reached at Nascent Soul; floating wreckage of a fallen heaven) - and small
    TRIAL GROUNDS opened by a token, each a fixed-size place like the World Seed's.

Q9. SWORD FLIGHT. Recommended: from Core Formation, a spirit sword in a new slot is ridden - free flight at
    several times walking pace for a steady drain of qi. Divinity flies without one. (The jetpack's flying is
    what it is built on.)

Q10. WITH THE OTHER MODS. It depends on none. Where another is on it meets it half way: Potioncraft's effects
     engine is what pills run on (that part is in the base game); Dimensions' places can have spirit veins;
     Rags to Riches villages can sell low-grade spirit stones. All by lists that skip what is not there.

Q11. ART. About 1,500 pictures. You called recolouring a weakness. Recommended: everything a player looks at
     for long is drawn fresh by script in the game's 16-pixel manner - the furnace, formation flags and cores,
     pills, talismans, swords, robes, the beasts, the herbs' signature stages, the UI - and recolouring is kept
     for honest families only (ten grades of the same spirit stone, the same herb at three ages).

Q12. DELIVERY. This cannot be one sitting. Recommended: eight stages (section 9), each playable, tested,
     documented, committed and pushed, with a word from you between them.

Q13. THE NAME. "Path of Immortals"? "Jade Heaven"? "Dao"? Yours to pick.

================================================================================================================
1. CULTIVATION
================================================================================================================

Each character has:
  REALM and STAGE     one of the fifty.
  QI                  how much they hold, against how much the stage holds. Full, they may attempt the next.
  SPIRIT ROOTS        five elements, each 0 to 100 (Q4).
  FOUNDATION          how well each realm was built: a quality from Flawed to Perfect, set at each breakthrough
                      by what was used for it. It multiplies everything after. A hurried cultivator gets there
                      sooner and is weaker for ever; this is the mod's long game.
  MERIDIANS           twelve, opened one by one in Qi Refining (each needs its own herb or pill): each opened
                      raises how fast qi moves, and three of them unlock technique slots.
  DANTIAN / CORE / SOUL   the thing each realm builds (2).
  COMPREHENSION       insights into each element and into the sword, the body, the pill, the formation:
                      gained by doing, needed for the higher techniques and for bottlenecks.
  MANUAL              the cultivation method in use (5).
  TECHNIQUES          what they know, and which are on the bar (6).

THE QI OF A PLACE. Every square of the world has a qi density, from a map made from the world's seed: thin on
the plains, richer on mountains, in old forests, deep underground and high in the sky, and richest of all along
SPIRIT VEINS - seams that run through the world like ore, glow faintly, and can be mined for spirit stones (which
kills the vein there) or sat on (which does not). The readout shows the qi where you stand. Formations gather
it; a spirit-stone pile adds to it while it lasts; a tribulation scorches it for days.

================================================================================================================
2. THE FIVE REALMS
================================================================================================================

QI REFINING I-X     Drawing qi into the body. Open the twelve meridians; fill the dantian.
                    Gains: a qi bar; the first techniques; spirit sight (see the qi of things).
                    Break through to Foundation with a FOUNDATION ESTABLISHMENT PILL - or without, at a third
                    of the odds and a Flawed foundation.

FOUNDATION          Turning qi to liquid; building the platform everything else stands on.
BUILDING I-X        Gains: hunger at half pace, twice the breath, a storage ring, a second technique row,
                    the Spirit Realm (Q8), the first tribulation.
                    The foundation's quality is fixed here by the grade of the pill, the qi of the place, and
                    how many of the ten stages were "tempered" (each stage has an optional harder task).

CORE FORMATION I-X  Condensing a golden core. The core has a grade (one to nine patterns), set by the
                    foundation's quality and by what is fed to it in the forming.
                    Gains: no need to eat; sword flight (Q9); a natal artifact that grows with you; the furnace
                    and formations of the third grade.

NASCENT SOUL I-X    The core breaks and a soul is born in it.
                    Gains: once a day, death is not death - the soul escapes to the last portal and the body
                    is remade; the soul can leave the body to scout (a free camera that can touch nothing);
                    the Immortal Ruins; soul techniques.

DIVINITY I-X        A domain of one's own: within a few squares, the cultivator's element rules - fire does not
                    burn them, water parts, plants grow, ore shows through rock.
                    Gains: flight without a sword; the domain; the last grades of everything.
                    X: the Ninefold Tribulation, and IMMORTALITY - an ending, a title, and a small set of
                    things only an immortal can make.

================================================================================================================
3. BREAKTHROUGHS, BOTTLENECKS, TRIBULATIONS
================================================================================================================

A SMALL BREAKTHROUGH (stage to stage) needs a full qi bar and a sitting. It has a chance - high early, lower
late - raised by pills, by the qi of the place, by formations, by a calm state (fed, rested, unhurt). Failure
costs a part of the bar and leaves "qi deviation": weakness for a while, cured by rest or a Calming Pill.

A BOTTLENECK is a stage that qi alone will not pass. There are three in every realm (at III, VI and IX), and
each asks for something that is not sitting still:
    an insight       comprehension of the right kind at the right level (found by using techniques, reading
                     steles in ruins, watching a tribulation, sitting through a storm or at a volcano's edge)
    a trial          kill a spirit beast of your own realm alone; survive a night in a secret realm
    a treasure       a particular herb of sufficient age, a beast core, a drop of spirit spring water
    a making         refine a pill, lay a formation, forge an artifact of the realm's grade yourself
Which three, for which stage, comes from the MANUAL being followed (5): two cultivators on different manuals
have different roads.

A GREAT BREAKTHROUGH (realm to realm) needs all of that at once - full qi at stage X, the realm's pill, the
realm's task done - and then the TRIBULATION: so many bolts of lightning over a minute or two, each stronger,
striking where the cultivator sits. What stands between them and it: a lightning-ward formation, talismans,
defensive techniques, robes, and health. Live, and the realm is theirs. Die, and it is the game's death with
the bar emptied and the foundation's quality marked down. The scorched ground left behind grows LIGHTNING HERBS,
which are worth the trouble by themselves.

================================================================================================================
4. HERBALISM, ALCHEMY, SPIRIT FOOD
================================================================================================================

SPIRIT HERBS (about 60 kinds, 12 to a realm). Each grows only where the qi is thick enough for it, and keeps
growing: a herb has an AGE - ten-year, hundred-year, thousand-year, ten-thousand-year - that it reaches by
being left alone in good qi (a game year is short; "years" here are a count of growth, and a spirit field
formation hurries it). Older is the same herb, several times as potent, and some recipes take nothing less.
Wild ones are found old; planted ones must be waited for. Each has a guardian, sometimes.
   Qi Refining: spirit grass, bloodvine, meridian root, dew lotus, ironbark fern ...
   Foundation:  jade ginseng, flame lily, frost orchid, earth-milk mushroom ...
   Core:        nine-leaf clover of the golden core, dragon's beard, purple cloud reishi ...
   Nascent:     soul-nurturing lotus, phoenix-tail grass, netherworld blossom ...
   Divinity:    the nine immortal herbs, each found in one place in a world.
Also: SPIRIT TREES (a peach that fruits every hundred days), SPIRIT SPRINGS (water that is itself an
ingredient), and SPIRIT FIELDS (soil that holds qi; a plot of it is the start of every sect).

ALCHEMY. The PILL FURNACE (five grades, each made of the next realm's metal) takes herbs and a flame and gives
pills. A recipe is known only once its PILL FORMULA has been found or bought. Every pill comes out at a grade -
low, middle, high, peak - by the alchemist's comprehension, the furnace, the flame and the herbs' ages; higher
grades are stronger and leave less PILL TOXIN (the reason not to live on pills: toxin slows cultivation until it
is worked off or a Cleansing Pill is taken). The flame matters: charcoal, then earth fire (a furnace set on a
magma vent), then a tamed SPIRIT FLAME - a thing caught in the wild and kept, of which there are nine.
   About 90 pills: qi gathering, meridian opening (12), breakthrough pills (the Foundation Establishment Pill and
   its four cousins), healing, antidotes, body tempering, comprehension, lifespan (if Q5), beast taming and
   feeding, root washing, and the poisons.

SPIRIT FOOD. A SPIRIT KITCHEN cooks spirit beasts and spirit crops (spirit rice, tea, peaches, wine) into meals
that give qi and long, mild effects with no toxin at all - slower than pills, and they stack with them. About 50
dishes; spirit tea ceremonies that raise comprehension when drunk in a fine place; spirit wine that ages.

================================================================================================================
5. MANUALS AND TECHNIQUES
================================================================================================================

A CULTIVATION MANUAL is the method a character cultivates by. One is in use at a time; changing is possible and
costs a stage. Each has a grade (Mortal, Earth, Heaven, Immortal), an element or two, a rate, a set of
bottleneck tasks, and things only it gives. About 20, of which a new character can find three or four Mortal
ones easily and will spend the game looking for better:
   Five Elements Breathing (plain, no weakness, slow)       Azure Wood Longevity Art (heals, grows things)
   Blazing Sun Scripture (fast, pill toxin builds faster)   Nine Springs Yin Art (strongest at night, in caves)
   Vajra Body Refining (cultivates the body, not qi: no spells, immense health)
   Thunder God Canon (cultivates in storms; tribulations feed it)   Sword Heart Sutra (one sword, nothing else) ...

TECHNIQUES are learned from TECHNIQUE SCROLLS (found, traded, copied at a writing desk if comprehension allows),
set on a technique bar, and cost qi. Each has a mastery that rises with use. About 120, in five elements and
four kinds:
   ATTACK      fire palm, vine lash, sword qi, ice needles, falling star; later, area and summoning arts
   DEFENCE     golden bell, water curtain, stone skin, the lightning-eating art
   MOVEMENT    cloud step (walk on air a moment), earth escape (pass through soil), shadow flash, sword flight
   UTILITY     spirit sight, object control (dig and place at a distance), rain calling, harvesting hand,
               the Art of the Spirit Farmer, alchemist's true fire, beast speech, sealing
Higher realms do not only add bigger numbers: earth escape changes how the underground is travelled, object
control changes building, the domain changes everything near.

Also: TALISMANS (paper, ink of beast blood, a brush: one-use techniques anyone can throw - the mod's flasks) and
ARTIFACTS (spirit swords, armour, bells, mirrors, banners, forged at a SPIRIT FORGE from spirit ores, each with
a grade and slots for beast cores; the natal artifact of a Core Formation cultivator is one of these, bound).

================================================================================================================
6. FORMATIONS
================================================================================================================

A formation is a pattern laid in the world: FORMATION FLAGS set at its points, a FORMATION CORE at its heart,
spirit stones in the core for fuel. Laid right, it lights - lines of qi drawn between the flags - and everything
inside it is under its rule until the stones run out. The pattern is read from a FORMATION DIAGRAM (found or
bought), which shows where the flags go; bigger and better formations want more flags, further apart, of higher
grades, in stricter shapes.
   Spirit Gathering (raises the qi inside: the first one everyone builds)   Spirit Field (herbs age faster)
   Concealment (creatures do not see what is inside)     Warding (hostile creatures cannot enter)
   Lightning Ward (takes a share of every bolt)          Five Elements Killing Array (hurts what enters)
   Illusion Maze (what enters wanders)                   Earth Fire (a furnace flame anywhere)
   Teleportation Array (two of them are a road; the Great one reaches other realms)
   Sect Protecting Grand Formation (all of the above, a screen wide, and it eats spirit stones)
About 25 formations in five grades. A formation master's comprehension lets them lay with fewer flags.

================================================================================================================
7. SPIRIT BEASTS
================================================================================================================

About 60 beasts, each of a realm, thicker where qi is thicker and far more dangerous than the game's creatures:
   Qi Refining: wind wolf, iron-back boar, spirit crane, jade rabbit, flame fox, stone ape ...
   Foundation:  thunder leopard, azure serpent, earth-armour tortoise, frost eagle ...
   Core:        golden-winged roc, nine-tailed fox, black tortoise spawn, qilin calf ...
   Nascent:     flood dragon, vermilion bird chick, white tiger ...
   Divinity:    the four sacred beasts themselves, one of each to a world, each guarding an immortal herb.
They drop BEAST CORES (alchemy, artifacts, formations), blood (talisman ink), hide, bone and meat (spirit food).
TAMING: a beast can be bound young (an egg or a cub, raised on beast pills) or beaten and contracted with a
Beast-Taming Talisman, if it is no more than a realm above its master. A contracted beast follows, fights,
grows as it is fed, can be ridden if it is big enough (the roc flies), and lives in a SPIRIT BEAST BAG when not
out. ROGUE CULTIVATORS and the remnant souls in ruins are the beasts that think.

================================================================================================================
8. THE WORLD, THE INTERFACE, THE ENGINE
================================================================================================================

IN THE WORLD: spirit veins and the qi map; spirit herbs by qi and biome; five spirit ores (one a realm) and
spirit jade; spirit springs; ruined sect halls, sealed cultivators' caves (built by the structure system
villages use) with manuals, formulas, diagrams and inheritances - an INHERITANCE being a dead cultivator's
whole road, given to whoever passes its trial; altars; steles that give insight once. In a world from before
the mod: nothing wild appears, so every staple has a second source (spirit stones condensed slowly from sitting;
seeds from a wandering spirit crane; the first manual from the portal).

INTERFACE (all new, and all named in the README rather than explained on screen):
   A QI BAR beside the others, and the realm and stage under the character's name.
   THE CULTIVATION SCREEN, from the character's panel: the body with its meridians and dantian, roots,
   foundation, comprehension, the manual and what the next step asks for - the one place the whole road is seen.
   A TECHNIQUE BAR above the inventory bar, keys of its own.
   THE FURNACE and THE FORMATION CORE have menus of their own (herbs, flame, grade; pattern, flags found, fuel).
   A BREAKTHROUGH is a sitting with a bar and a chance shown beforehand; a tribulation counts its bolts.
   Qi density on the readout; spirit sight as a view that tints the world by qi.

ENGINE (as data fields the base game and other mods could use; no "if the mod is on"):
   per-character cultivation state, saved (a new save version); the qi map; stages and realms as data
   (a "path" file: every stage, what it holds, gives and asks, so another mod could add a different path);
   item quality grades and ages (a number kept with one item, as meters are); techniques as a new kind of
   definition, with a bar and keys; formations as patterns matched in the world; breakthroughs and tribulations;
   creature realms, taming, following, mounts; conditional sources ("known formulas"); the screens.
   It builds on what the last few mods added: effects (pills), other places (secret realms), structures
   (ruins), signals' device list (formations), spells (talismans).

10. REVISION - THE OWNER'S ANSWERS, AND WHAT THEY ADD   (the plan above stands except where this changes it)

ANSWERS. Q1-Q9, Q11: as recommended. Q10: it need not be made to work with the other mods. Q12: one sitting,
no stops; the owner reviews at the end. Q13: the mod is SEEKER OF THE DAO.

TIERS. Cultivation starts at tier I, unlike Dimensions, and the whole of the trade and the portals follow it:

    Qi Refining          tiers I and II        Nascent Soul     tiers VII and VIII
    Foundation Building  tiers III and IV      Divinity         tiers IX and X
    Core Formation       tiers V and VI        Immortality      tier XI

Everything the mod adds has a tier by the realm it belongs to (the lower of the pair for what the first half of
a realm makes, the higher for the second). THE PORTAL AND THE TRADE PORTAL GO ON TO XI: five more levels of
each, hung on the end of the game's six, raised with the spirit crystal of Nascent Soul (VII, VIII), of Divinity
(IX, X) and with an immortal crystal (XI). Each new portal level makes something of its own.

THREE TRADES MORE.
  SPIRIT MINING   Spirit ore, one metal to a realm (spirit iron, jadesteel, starmetal, voidsilver, heavenly
                  gold), each too hard for the pick before it; spirit stone veins in five grades, which are
                  money, fuel for formations and the source of a place's qi; spirit jade. Spirit picks; a
                  technique that shows ore through rock; veins that are worth more left alone than dug.
  BLACKSMITHING   The SPIRIT FORGE, five grades: each realm's metal into tools, weapons and armour - and into
                  the next grade of every bench the mod has.
  ARTIFICING      The ARTIFICER'S TABLE, five grades: ARTIFACTS - things carried in the artifact slots (the
                  four slots relics use) that do something while they are there: bells, mirrors, gourds,
                  banners, seals, lamps. Each takes a beast core and belongs to a set.

ENDGAME. For an Immortal:
  - SETS with effects that are not just bigger numbers: of robes (four pieces) and of artifacts (four pieces),
    one of each for every realm and three at the end - the Azure Dragon set (the air holds you up; breath never
    runs out), the Vermilion Phoenix set (fire and magma do nothing; once dead, not dead), the Black Tortoise
    set (most of every blow turned; tribulation lightning mostly spent on it), the White Tiger set (every blow
    half again; ore and beasts shown through rock).
  - CHALLENGES. Heaven's Trials: three sealed arenas reached by a token, each with one thing in it to beat.
    The Four Sacred Beasts, one of each to a world. The Heavenly Tribulation can be CALLED DOWN again with a
    talisman, on purpose, for what it leaves behind (heaven-tempered essence: the only source of tier XI
    materials). And the last manual, which can only be written by an Immortal.

OTHER PLACES - each unlike anything the game has:
  THE WORLD TREE (the Spirit Realm, from Foundation Building). Not land at all: trunks as wide as houses rising
      the whole height of the place out of a lake, with boughs for ground, leaves for hills, hollows in the wood
      with springs in them, and vines to climb between. Everything in it is alive and thick with qi; nothing
      there is stone.
  THE FALLEN HEAVEN (from Nascent Soul). A palace that fell: halls, pagodas and broken bridges of white jade
      on slabs that hang in a sky with no sun, only stars. What is left of its treasuries is still in it, and
      so is what guards them.
  HEAVEN'S TRIALS. Three small sealed places, each one arena.

WHAT "ONE SITTING" COSTS. Sections 1-8 describe more than one sitting can build. Where the mod as built falls
short of them, the README and section 11 (added when it is done) will say exactly where.


11. WHAT WAS BUILT, AND WHAT WAS NOT

Built, in one sitting (counts are what the mod adds: 180 blocks, 369 items, 274 recipes, 17 creatures, 13 biomes,
6 dimensions, 17 structures, 11 sets):
  - The road: five realms of ten stages and immortality; qi from meditating, by place, manual, roots, meridians,
    foundation and toxin; breakthroughs with a chance; bottlenecks at the 3rd, 6th and 9th stage of each realm;
    realm pills; tribulations (3, 6, 9, 12 and 36 bolts) that only wards turn; foundations set by how the
    tribulation was come through; a Nascent Soul's one survival of death; the button and the cultivation screen.
  - Alchemy (pill furnace I-V, 23 pills), Herbalism (10 herbs and 3 crops gated by qi; spirit soil and spirit stone
    blocks to raise it), Spirit Mining (4 grades of spirit stone, cinnabar, jade, 5 ores, 2 crystals), Blacksmithing
    (spirit forge I-V; six metals: tools, swords, armour), Artificing (artificer's table I-V; 13 artifacts in two
    sets, flying swords), Formations (3 flags, 10 arrays: gathering x3, verdant, killing, sword, swift, protection,
    teleport, tribulation), Techniques (18, costing qi), Talismans (13), Manuals (11), Spirit food (10 dishes),
    Spirit beasts (13, and the Four Sacred Beasts), robes of the five realms.
  - Portals and trade portals VII-XI, with tiers by realm as asked.
  - Places: ruined sects and immortals' caves in the world as it began; the World Tree (a new "grove" generator);
    the Fallen Heaven (islands with ruins and broken bridges built on them); Heaven's Trials (four courts, not
    three: one to each Sacred Beast).
  - For an immortal: the called tribulation and heaven-tempered essence, chaos iron, the four trials, four sets of
    armour with set powers, the Four Symbols seal, the Dao of Heaven.

Chosen where the plan left it open: techniques live in the ordinary bar (no technique bar); artifacts use the
relic slots; manuals are studied one at a time (the last studied is the one in use); herb "ages" became a qi
threshold per herb, not a count of years; pill grades became the furnace's grade; a formation's flags must all be
the same distance from its heart.

Not built, or thinner than sections 1-8 describe:
  - No sects to join, no disciples, missions, reputation or NPC cultivators. The ruins are empty of people.
  - No dual cultivation, no demonic path, no karma, no heart demons beyond the chance of failure.
  - Spirit beasts cannot be tamed, bonded, ridden or raised - only fought and caged. The Sacred Beasts fight as
    other creatures do (they close and strike); they have no attacks of their own.
  - Techniques are the spells the engine already had (damage, dig, shield, grow, freeze, hold, weather, time, and
    effects on oneself) at a qi cost. There are no sword-flight duels, no technique levels or mastery.
  - Formations are one ring of flags; there are no multi-ring arrays, no arrays that hide or trap a character.
  - Alchemy has no pill-refining minigame, no furnace explosions, no pill quality: a recipe makes what it makes.
  - Artifacts do not level, bind or awaken. No storage rings.
  - The elements do not act on each other (no generating or overcoming cycle): a root only speeds a manual of
    its element.
  - The Fallen Heaven's sky is a colour, not stars. The trial courts are bare of anything but lamps.
  - Balance is untested by play: every number (qi per stage, pill strengths, bolt shares, prices) is a first guess.
  - Not compatible with other mods by the owner's leave, but it clashes in name with none of them.

    techniques. Saved (version 38).
  - Taming: beast-taming talismans in five grades; a tamed beast follows and fights.
  - The twelve meridians each with a pill of its own herb, opened in order.
  - Pills in every strength, refined and peak pills from old herbs, poisons, insight pills, a second road to each
    realm's pill; 50 more dishes; 9 more manuals; 100 more techniques (twenty to an element); 25 more talismans;
    16 more formations (among them the sect-protecting grand formation); 45 more creatures, rogue cultivators
    among them; storage rings, the nine flames, four more sets of artifacts, five sets of leather armour, spears,
    sabres, needles, bows; columns, stairs, smooth and carved stone; furniture, steles, spirit springs and wells;
    copying of manuals and techniques.
  - The bench menu shows a great many recipes in rows (for every bench in the game).

Still short of the plan, said plainly:
  - Items are 1,084 against about 1,150 and recipes 1,149 against about 1,400. I stopped where the next hundred
    would have been padding - the same thing under another name - rather than content.
  - Recipes are not "known only once a formula is found": everything can be made by whoever has the bench.
  - Pills do not come out at a grade by the alchemist's skill; strength is by the recipe (plain, refined, peak).
  - Techniques have no mastery and there is no technique bar: they are in the ordinary bar. The 100 added are the
    engine's existing effects in rising strengths, twenty names to an element - a family, not 100 different ideas.
    Earth escape, object control and the soul leaving the body are not there.
  - Insight is one number, not one for each element and craft.
  - A tamed beast cannot be ridden, does not grow, and has no bag; fliers and swimmers cannot be tamed.
  - No natal artifact, no core grades, no tempering tasks for each stage, no domain beyond what Divinity gives.
  - No formation diagrams: a formation is its heart and one ring of flags. No illusion maze.
  - No qi map of the world from its seed, and no spirit sight view: qi is by depth, place, biome and what is near.
  - The Sacred Beasts still have no attacks of their own; no inheritances; no sects to join.
  - The herb plants are small simple drawings; the 100 new technique slips differ only in colour and marks.
  - Nothing has been played: every number is a guess. Taming, the steles, the furniture and the three-row bench
    menu were looked at in a screenshot or driven in a test, not used in play.


13. THE REST OF THE PLAN

Asked for every piece of function listed as missing, and built (608 blocks, 1,125 items, 1,322 recipes now):
understanding by element and by craft; tempering, core grades, natal artifacts, the domain, the soul's sight; a
manual changed at the cost of a stage and death at the cost of qi; formulas and diagrams; skill, furnace and flame
giving more from a making, and flames set down by the furnace; techniques learned onto a row of their own with
keys and mastery, and arts of movement; sword flight spending qi; double formations, warding that bars, the
illusion maze, herbs feeling formations; fliers and swimmers tamed, beasts fed and ridden, the beast bag; the
Sacred Beasts striking from afar with an immortal herb in each court; qi by the seed and spirit veins; spirit sight
and the qi readout; inheritances; a sect as a mission board with merit and five ranks; stars; other sources for
old worlds.

Where what was built is less than the plan's words:
  - Pill "grade" is how many come out, not a stronger pill; a flame is set beside the furnace, not under it.
  - The sect is a board, not people: no elders, rivals or missions that are given - only things handed in.
  - The technique keys are Shift and a number, fixed; they are not in the key-bindings menu.
  - A rider is drawn standing in the beast rather than seated on it; riding is not saved (they are on foot after
    a reload). A swimmer that is tamed stays in its water.
  - The domain does two things (growth, harm), not the plan's four; object control digs at a distance but does
    not place; the soul's scouting uncovers the map and no more.
  - Core grade does not depend on what is "fed to it in the forming" beyond the pill's toxin.
  - Items 1,125 of about 1,150 and recipes 1,322 of about 1,400.
  - Tested by test and by a few screenshots (the screen, the technique row, spirit sight, the stars); mounts,
    the board, strikes, the maze and the three-row menu with a recipe chosen have not been seen in play.
