SKYBLOCK   (a mod laid over the game; asked for as "a skyblock mod with features that allow every resource
typically gainable to be gained somehow", to be built at once - so what would have been questions were decided)

WHAT WAS DECIDED
   - It is an ordinary mod, on top of the game and of any other mod: not a game of its own.
   - With it on a NEW world is empty sky: one island eleven squares long (grass, dirt, a core of stone, an oak,
     the spawn portal), ten small islands 45 to 90 squares apart to either side (sand, ore, clay with a golden
     chest, marble, ice, gold), and a floor at the bottom that nothing reaches. Worlds made before are untouched.
   - "Every resource" is not a list written by hand. The generator works out, from the data, every traded item
     that could NOT be come by starting from a tree and the dirt it stands in, and gives exactly those - first
     what no recipe makes at all, then the cheapest thing that unties each knot that is left. For the game that
     is 53 things of 298; the rest follow by the game's own recipes.
   - The same is done for every mod the game comes with, as recipes marked "optional" (not there when the mod is
     off). Dungeon loot is left out: the dungeons can still be gone into.
   - Nothing comes from nothing but DUST: the cloud condenser makes sky dust with no inputs, slowly; dust makes
     star grit, grit makes void essence. Everything given is paid for in one of the three, by its price.

BUILT (tools/mod_skyblock.py; engine: `"skyblock"` in worldgen.json and `"spring"` on a block; test_skyblock_mod)
   - Five benches, made at the workbench: cloud condenser, sieve (a chance at one of the things of a price
     band), lodestone table (the game's own things for certain, at four times the price), hatchery (a caged
     creature - every one of the game's 26, and by chance each mod's), composter (sticks or wood to dirt).
   - A well: shut in by a block either side and one under it, it fills by itself. Water is otherwise never made.
   - 7 blocks, 9 items, 152 recipes (93 for the game, 59 for the mods), 7 structures, 49 quests in a tree.
   - The test works the reach out again in C and fails naming anything of the game's that cannot be come by.

NOT BUILT, AND WHAT IS NOT COVERED
   - "Can be come by" is reckoned from recipes alone. It does not check that every BENCH on the way can be had
     (benches are raised with things that are themselves covered, but nothing proves the chain), nor biomes:
     a creature or crop that wants a certain country is given, but may not thrive on an island in the plains.
   - Blocks that are not items, and things only ever found as a place (a pole, a village), are not given.
   - Mods' things are given by chance only, forty to a pool: a particular one may take many goes.
   - With very many mods on, the recipes may pass the engine's limit of 4,096. Not measured.
   - No falling into the void: characters will not step off, as in the game. Things dropped over the edge are
     lost on the floor.
   - Never played. Costs follow prices; how long anything takes is a guess.
