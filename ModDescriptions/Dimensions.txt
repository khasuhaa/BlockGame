DIMENSIONS - PLAN  (draft for approval; nothing below is built yet)

A mod that adds two whole other worlds to a save, reached through portals that have to be worked up to:

    Overworld (tiers I-VI)  ->  the AETHER (tiers VII-VIII)  ->  the DEEP (tiers IX-X)

The Aether is nothing but flying islands over a sea of cloud. The Deep is nothing but rock, with caverns many
times the size of the overworld's. Each has its own biomes, blocks, ores, gems, trees, creatures, benches,
mechanics and a full set of things to make - aimed at about as much content each as the base game has
(base game today: 356 blocks, 427 items, 505 recipes, 9 biomes, 26 creatures, 16 trees).

Names below are proposals. Anything can be renamed, cut or swapped before I start.

================================================================================================================
0. QUESTIONS I NEED ANSWERED FIRST
================================================================================================================

Q1. WORKBENCH V AND VI CLASH WITH TWO EXISTING MODS.
    Industry already adds a Workbench V and VI, and Magic and Mayhem a Workbench V, under the names workbench_5 /
    workbench_6. Two mods that define the same name cannot be on together, so as things stand Dimensions could not
    share a playset with either. Options:
      A (recommended). A small engine addition: a mod can ADD a tier to the end of a bench's chain without
         redefining the tier below. Dimensions' tiers get names of their own ("Aether workbench", "Deep workbench")
         and are bolted on after whatever the top workbench is in that playset. Its recipes ask for "the Aether
         workbench or better", not "level 5", so Industry's Workbench V cannot be used to skip the Aether. They are
         still shown as Workbench V / VI when Dimensions is the only mod on.
      B. Dimensions cannot be on with Industry or Magic and Mayhem. Simplest, but a pity for a mod this size.
    The same addition lets me add tiers to the tool bench, furnace, press and the rest without clashing with any
    future mod.

Q2. WHO IS SIMULATED. A world can have two characters. If they are in different dimensions, do both dimensions
    run at once? Recommended: only the dimension of the character being played runs; the other is frozen (its
    character, its machines, its crops) until you switch to them. Running two or three worlds at once is possible
    but triples the cost of a tick and the work of this mod's engine half.

Q3. SIZE AND PLACE. Recommended: each dimension is as wide as the overworld and lines up with it one to one - a
    portal at column 1,200 comes out at column 1,200. (The alternative is a "one block there is eight here" rule;
    I do not think it earns its confusion.)

Q4. FALLING OUT OF THE AETHER. Recommended: fall off the bottom of the Aether and you come out of the overworld's
    sky over the same column, and had better have a glider, a jetpack or water under you. It makes the islands
    dangerous without being a death sentence, and gives the glider (below) a job from the first minute.

Q5. LAVA. A real second liquid that flows like water is a large piece of engine work on its own. Recommended:
    the Deep ships with MAGMA as still pools and falls that are placed by the world generator and by a bucket,
    burn whatever touches them and light the caves - and flowing lava is a later addition if you want it.

Q6. HARDNESS. Today any pickaxe digs anything, only slower. To make "you cannot mine zephyrite with a stone
    pick" true I would add a hardness to blocks and a tier to tools (engine, data-driven, used only by this mod's
    blocks unless you want it on the base ores too). Yes / no?

Q7. BOSSES. Two portal ingredients below come from big, rare, dangerous creatures (the Thunderbird, the Crystal
    Golem). They would be the game's first "boss" fights - still ordinary creatures to the engine, just large,
    with a lot of health and their own attack. Keep, or make those ingredients craftable instead?

Q8. THE PORTAL X ITEM. See 4.6 - I propose the Pocket Portal, with two alternatives.

Q9. ART. About 1,000 new pictures. They would be generated by a script in the game's 16-pixel style, as the other
    mods' were, with hand-tuned palettes per dimension. Shout if you would rather supply some yourself.

Q10. DELIVERY. This is far too much for one sitting. I propose six stages (section 7), each ending playable,
    tested, committed and pushed, so you can try it and steer between stages.

================================================================================================================
1. HOW IT PLAYS, START TO FINISH
================================================================================================================

  1. Finish the overworld: Portal VI, Workbench IV, titanium, carbon fiber, electricity.
  2. Build the AETHER PORTAL (crafting tree in 2.1). It only opens when placed high up - on one of the floating
     islands, 80 or more above the ground - so the last step of the overworld is building your way into the sky.
  3. The Aether: gather skysteel, galvanite, aerite and moonsilver; find ZEPHYRITE (tier VII gem) and HELIODOR
     (tier VIII gem); raise the portal to VII and VIII, the trade portal to VII and VIII, the workbench to V, and
     every other bench by one or two tiers.
  4. Build the DEEP PORTAL from Aether materials (tree in 3.1). It only opens when placed at the very bottom of the
     overworld, within 20 of the floor.
  5. The Deep: deepiron, cinderite, lumite, adamant; UMBRITE (tier IX gem) and EMBERHEART (tier X gem); portal and
     trade portal IX and X, Workbench VI, another tier or two on every bench.
  6. Portal X makes the last pickaxe and the POCKET PORTAL.

2. THE AETHER
================================================================================================================

2.1 THE AETHER PORTAL - CRAFTING TREE  (everything here is made in the overworld)

    Aether portal        Workbench IV: 1 aether keystone, 8 skyglass, 6 featherweight alloy, 4 marble column - 10m
      Aether keystone    Portal VI: 1 gem prism, 1 storm capacitor, 1 diamond block - 5m
        Gem prism        Press III: 1 each of amethyst, sapphire, emerald, ruby, diamond - 3m
        Storm capacitor  Craft bench IV: 1 flywheel, 20 copper wire, 2 silicon wafer, 1 gold block - 5m;
                         then CHARGED: set down and fed 50,000 units of electricity (a new "charges" rule for
                         blocks - the first thing in the game that stores a charge and is picked up charged)
      Skyglass (x2)      Electric kiln: 2 glass, 1 diamond shard, 1 crushed limestone - 1m
      Featherweight alloy  Electric furnace: 1 titanium ingot, 1 carbon fiber sheet, 1 gold ingot - 2m
    Placed 80 or more above the ground it opens: a 3-tall arch like the other portals, sky-blue in white stone.
    Lower than that it stands dark, and says nothing (the README will).
    Stepping through takes the character, and what they carry, to the same column of the Aether; a return portal
    is made there the first time, on the nearest island (or on a small platform of cloudstone if there is none).

2.2 THE WORLD
    No ground. Islands of every size from a few blocks to a hundred across, at every height, most thickly in a band
    through the middle; under everything a CLOUD SEA (a floor of cloud blocks with lakes of real water in it) and
    under that the void (Q4). Days are a third longer and nights brighter than below. Islands hang in strings and
    clusters so that a glider and a few bridges get you about; the big ones have caves through them.

    Biomes (each a kind of island, with its own surface, trees, plants, ores and creatures):
      Cloudmeadow    - grass and wildflowers; the gentle starting islands; fleecelings, moas
      Skywood        - forested islands of windwood and sky-willow; hanging vines of sky-kelp
      Stormreach     - bare dark rock under permanent thunder; galvanite; lightning (2.6); gale hawks, the Thunderbird
      Crystal spires - tall thin islands of skymarble and glass-like crystal; zephyrite
      Sunpetal gardens - warm golden islands, giant flowers as trees; heliodor near the top of the world
      Frostveil      - iced islands high up; thin air (2.6); moonsilver
      The ruins      - broken halls of a people who lived here: ruin brick, pillars, loot (and, with Discovery Road
                       on, relic chests with a pool of their own)
      Cloud sea      - the floor: cloud, cloud lakes, cloud-reeds; aether carp, sky jellies

2.3 BLOCKS (about 140)
    Stone and earth: aetherstone, cloudstone, skymarble, moonrock, sky soil, cloud grass, storm slate, frost glass,
      ruin brick (+ mossy, cracked), solar tile - each with its column and stairs, as the base stones have
    Cloud: cloud (walk through it slowly), dense cloud (stand on it), storm cloud (bounces you - a trampoline),
      rain cloud (waters the crops under it)
    Ores: skysteel ore, galvanite ore, aerite ore, moonsilver ore (glows at night; only yields at night),
      zephyrite and heliodor (as gems grow below: on cave floors and walls), and each ore as a block, column, stairs
    Trees (8): windwood, sky-willow, sunpetal (giant flower), frostpine, cloud-oak, amberleaf, storm birch,
      star-apple - logs, leaves, planks, and a fruit or seed each
    Plants and crops (about 20): skyberry, cloud-reed, sunpetal, windwheat, star-apple, frost mint, aether cotton,
      glowcap, moon lily (two-tone, like tulips), sky-kelp, thunder pepper ...
    Things: Aether portal, skyglass, lightning rod, wind turbine, cloud loom, skyforge, rain barrel, glider rack,
      nest box, sky lantern, wind chime (a block), banner poles, ruin pillars, hanging baskets

2.4 MATERIALS
    Skysteel      - light and hard: the tier VII metal. Tools, armour, rails that weigh nothing.
    Galvanite     - holds a charge: wire that loses nothing, batteries, the storm capacitor's big brother.
    Aerite        - floats: blocks of it stay up with nothing under them (a new block rule), gliders, balloons.
    Moonsilver    - the tier VIII metal: tools that are fastest at night, armour that glows.
    Zephyrite (gem, VII), Heliodor (gem, VIII) - with shards, blocks, chandeliers, dodos and eggs like the others.
    Cloud cloth (from fleeceling wool on the cloud loom), storm glass, feathers, stardust, sky jelly, wind essence.

2.5 CREATURES (14)
    Fleeceling        - a sheep made of cloud; shorn for cloud wool (uses the game's shearing)
    Moa               - a big ground bird of the meadows; lays eggs; can be ridden (first mount: uses the vehicle
                        code - you "get on" and steer with A and D, and it hops gaps)
    Cloud ray         - drifts between islands, harmless; its hide makes gliders
    Sky jelly         - floats over the cloud sea, stings (poison); drops sky jelly
    Gale hawk         - hostile, dives on you from above the way a dropbear drops
    Storm wisp        - a ball of lightning in Stormreach; hurts to touch, drops wind essence
    Star moth         - night only, harmless, drops stardust
    Aether carp, silverfin - fish of the cloud lakes
    Zephyrite dodo, Heliodor dodo - the egg tree goes on: diamond dodo -> zephyrite -> heliodor
    Ruin sentinel     - a stone guardian that stands still in the ruins until you take something
    Harpy bat         - cave dweller of the big islands
    THE THUNDERBIRD   - Stormreach, rare. Large, a lot of health, calls lightning. Drops the storm feather the
                        Deep portal needs (Q7)

2.6 NEW MECHANICS
    Gliding          - a GLIDER in the chest slot: step off an edge and you sail, losing one block of height for
                       every four across, further with the wind behind you. (Uses the flying code the jetpack
                       has, with a downward drift.) The way to get from island to island before you can bridge.
    Wind             - blows one way or the other, changing with the weather; pushes gliders, turns turbines.
    Lightning        - in a storm, strikes the highest thing about. A lightning rod takes it and turns it into a
                       great lump of electricity; an unsheltered character takes it instead.
    Thin air         - high up, the environment score runs down as it does in the cold below; sky clothes keep it.
    Floating blocks  - aerite and anything "buoyant" needs no support: sky bridges, hanging gardens.
    Bouncing         - storm cloud throws you up three blocks.
    Rain clouds      - a block that waters what is under it; crops under one grow half again as fast.
    Wind power       - turbines make electricity by the wind; more the higher they stand.
    Charged things   - blocks and items that hold electricity and keep it when picked up (capacitors, batteries).

2.7 BENCHES
    New:  Skyforge (smelts by wind and lightning: all Aether metals), Cloud loom (cloth, sails, gliders),
          Wind turbine and Lightning rod (power), Aerie (hatches and keeps moas and hawks), Stargazer's table
          (night-only recipes: moonsilver work, stardust), Rain barrel (water without a bucket)
    New tiers of benches the game already has, each with recipes of its own:
          Workbench V (5 skysteel ingots)          Tool bench VIII (skysteel), IX (moonsilver)
          Portal VII, VIII                          Trade portal VII, VIII
          Furnace IV (aetherstone lining)           Metalwork bench III
          Armor bench IV (skysteel, cloudweave), V (moonsilver)
          Craft bench V                             Press IV
          Tailor's bench III (cloud cloth)          Woodwork bench II (the eight sky woods)
          Builder's bench II (every Aether stone as brick, tile, column, stairs)
          Kiln II (skyglass, storm glass)           Mixing bench II, Dye bench II (four sky pigments)
          Campfire -> Sky hearth (sky food)         Train yard II (skysteel rail: faster; the airship, below)

2.8 THINGS TO MAKE (about 180 items, 250 recipes) - the headings, not every line
    Tools: skysteel and moonsilver pickaxe, axe, spade, machete, sword; zephyrite and heliodor pickaxes; storm bow;
      grappling hook (pulls you to a block up to 8 away); cloud bottle; weather vane; spyglass (zooms the view out)
    Worn: skysteel armour, moonsilver armour, cloudweave clothes (thin-air proof), glider, wind boots (hop further),
      storm cloak, feather cap - each with a 3D worn picture like the base armour
    Travel: glider, balloon (lifts a chest), sky rail, AIRSHIP (a vehicle on no rails at all: fuelled, steered with
      the keys, carries two and a chest - the end of Aether travel)
    Food: about 25 - skyberry tart, cloud candy, moa roast, star-apple pie, thunder pepper stew, sky tea ...
    Building: about 60 - every stone and wood as blocks, columns, stairs, doors, windows, lanterns, banners
    Power: galvanite wire, storm battery, wind turbine, lightning rod, charged capacitor

3. THE DEEP
================================================================================================================

3.1 THE DEEP PORTAL - CRAFTING TREE  (made in the Aether and the overworld)

    Deep portal          Workbench V: 1 deep keystone, 8 moonrock, 6 moonsilver ingot, 4 aerite block - 15m
      Deep keystone      Portal VIII: 1 gravity core, 1 heliodor block, 1 storm feather - 10m
        Gravity core     Skyforge: 4 aerite ingot, 4 skysteel block, 1 charged storm battery - 10m
                         (aerite turned inside out: where aerite floats, this sinks through anything)
        Storm feather    from the Thunderbird (Q7) - or, if no bosses: Stargazer's table, 20 stardust, 10 feathers
      Moonrock, moonsilver, aerite block - gathered and made in the Aether
    Placed within 20 of the bottom of the overworld it opens: dull red in black stone. Higher, it stands dark.

3.2 THE WORLD
    All rock, top to bottom, with a roof you cannot dig through and no sky. No sun and no moon: the only light is
    what glows. The caves are caverns - halls thirty and forty blocks high, a hundred long, opening into each other
    - with rivers, lakes, magma, and in places a floor so far down it is lost in the dark. The deeper, the hotter.

    Biomes:
      Fungal forest   - giant mushrooms for trees, glowing spores; the gentle starting caves
      Glowmoss grotto - walls and floors of light; crops grow here without sun
      Crystal cathedral - enormous geodes: lumite, umbrite on the walls
      Bone hollows    - fossil beds: ribs like arches, bonestone, things to dig up
      Sunken lake     - an underground sea of real water, blind fish, islands with their own caves
      Obsidian flats  - black glass plains between magma falls
      Magma fields    - pools and falls of magma, cinderite, the heat (3.6)
      The Roots       - the bottoms of the overworld's trees, grown huge: rootwood, amber
      The abyss       - the very bottom: adamant and emberheart, and what guards them

3.3 BLOCKS (about 150)
    Stone: deepslate, obsidian, magmarock, lumerock (glows), bonestone, ashstone, dripstone, black glass,
      cinder brick, deep tile - each with column and stairs
    Fungus and wood: eight giant mushrooms (caps and stems as "leaves" and "logs"): bluecap, emberstool, pale giant,
      ink morel, lantern fungus, shelf-wood, rootwood, bonewood - planks, and a spore or cap to eat or plant
    Ores: deepiron ore, cinderite ore (warm to stand by), lumite crystal, adamant ore, umbrite and emberheart (gems)
    Hazards: magma (still), magma fall, gas vent (bursts into flame if a torch is near), loose rock (falls when
      what is under it goes - the game's first falling block), thin crust (breaks under you over a drop)
    Plants and crops (about 20): glowcap, cave rice, blindroot, ember pepper, moss melon, lichen, deep kelp,
      crystal bloom (two-tone), spore pod, stoneberry ...
    Things: Deep portal, magma forge, crystal lathe, fungal vat, geothermal generator, drill rig, heat shield,
      lumite lamp, bone furniture, cave lift

3.4 MATERIALS
    Deepiron      - the tier IX metal: heavy, slow tools that never wear and hit hard.
    Cinderite     - burns forever: a fuel that is not used up; warm blocks; the magma forge.
    Lumite        - light you can carry: lamps with no fuel, lenses, the crystal lathe's stock.
    Umbral steel  - deepiron and umbrite: the tier IX armour, dark and quiet (creatures see you from less far).
    Adamant       - the tier X metal, from the abyss only.
    Umbrite (gem, IX), Emberheart (gem, X) - with shards, blocks, chandeliers, dodos and eggs.
    Chitin, bone, amber, spore dust, cave silk, magma glass, fossil.

3.5 CREATURES (14)
    Glow beetle      - harmless, lights the fungal forest; kept in lanterns
    Cave crawler     - hostile, many-legged, climbs walls and ceilings (walks on any surface: new movement)
    Deep bat         - hangs from the roof and drops, as dropbears do from trees
    Blind cavefish, lantern eel - fish of the sunken lake
    Magma slug       - lives in magma; leaves a trail of cinder; drops cinderite
    Spore puffer     - bursts when hit, poisoning what is near
    Tunnel mole      - harmless digger; tamed with blindroot, it follows and digs soft rock for you
    Rock worm        - comes up out of the floor under you
    Fungus strider   - tall, slow, rideable (a second mount: steps over three-block drops)
    Umbral stalker   - only moves where there is no light; a torch stops it dead
    Umbrite dodo, Emberheart dodo - the end of the egg tree
    Bone hound       - packs in the bone hollows
    THE CRYSTAL GOLEM - Crystal cathedral, rare. Drops the golem core that Workbench VI needs (Q7)

3.6 NEW MECHANICS
    Heat             - the environment score runs down near magma and in the lower caves; cooling clothes keep it,
                       ordinary warm ones make it worse. (The cold of the overworld, turned over: a "hot" place
                       and a "cooling" share on clothes, beside "cold" and "warmth".)
    True dark        - no sun, ever: light is something you bring, grow or build. Lumite lamps need no fuel.
    Falling rock     - loose rock comes down when undermined. Dig carefully; or use it.
    Gas              - vents of it burn when lit: a danger with a torch in hand, and a free furnace once piped.
    Magma            - burns, lights, and is fuel: a bucket of it runs a magma forge for an hour.
    Geothermal power - a generator on a hot block makes electricity for ever.
    Digging machines - the drill rig: a vehicle on rails that mines what is in front of it into its own chest.
    Fossils          - dug up in pieces and put together at the crystal lathe into trophies - and one or two that
                       are more than trophies.
    Taming           - the tunnel mole and the fungus strider: fed, they are yours (uses the caged-creature rules).

3.7 BENCHES
    New:  Magma forge (all Deep metals; fuelled by magma or cinderite), Crystal lathe (lumite, gems, fossils),
          Fungal vat (ferments: drinks with effects for a while - the game's first timed buffs), Geothermal
          generator, Drill rig, Bone carver, Heat shield (cools the squares round it)
    New tiers:
          Workbench VI (golem core, 5 deepiron)     Tool bench X (deepiron), XI (umbral), XII (adamant)
          Portal IX, X                              Trade portal IX, X
          Furnace V (cinder brick)                  Metalwork bench IV
          Armor bench VI (deepiron, umbral), VII (adamant)
          Craft bench VI                            Press V (makes gem blocks of the four new gems, magma glass)
          Tailor's bench IV (cave silk, chitin)     Woodwork bench III (the eight fungus woods)
          Builder's bench III                       Kiln III
          Mixing bench III, Dye bench III           Electric benches Mk II (twice the speed on galvanite wire)

3.8 THINGS TO MAKE (about 190 items, 260 recipes)
    Tools: deepiron, umbral and adamant pickaxe, axe, spade, machete, sword; umbrite and emberheart pickaxes;
      rock hammer (breaks a 3x3), lumite lantern (held light), magma bucket, seismic charge (clears a room),
      prospector's lens (shows ore through 5 blocks of rock)
    Worn: deepiron armour, umbral armour, adamant armour, heat suit, miner's helmet (a light on your head - the
      game's first light you wear), chitin plate, moleskin boots
    Travel: cave lift, drill rig, strider saddle, rope and piton (climb down any drop - the answer to "nobody
      lets themselves fall")
    Food and drink: about 25, with the fungal vat's drinks giving a boon for a few minutes (speed, sight in the
      dark, cool, strength) - uses the boons Discovery Road brought in, on a timer
    Building: about 60

4. WHAT THE PORTALS AND TRADE PORTALS GET
================================================================================================================

4.1 Portal VII    upgrade: 1 zephyrite.   Makes: zephyrite pickaxe; SKY ANCHOR (a block you can teleport to from
                                          any portal, like a portal with no menu - cheap waypoints for islands)
4.2 Portal VIII   upgrade: 1 heliodor.    Makes: heliodor pickaxe; the deep keystone (3.1)
4.3 Portal IX     upgrade: 1 umbrite.     Makes: umbrite pickaxe; RECALL STONE (one use: takes you to the last
                                          portal you used, from anywhere in any dimension)
4.4 Portal X      upgrade: 1 emberheart.  Makes: emberheart pickaxe; and 4.6
4.5 Trade portal VII-X, each for the tier's gem, coins and crystals on the same rising scale as I-VI. Everything
    in this mod has a category and a tier VII-X, so the Aether's things are bought and sold at a tier VII or VIII
    trade portal and the Deep's at IX or X. No new categories.
4.6 THE PORTAL X ITEM - proposal: the POCKET PORTAL.
    Made at Portal X: 1 emberheart, 1 heliodor, 1 portal VI, 1 gravity core. Kept in the bar. Selected and
    clicked, it opens the portal's own menu wherever you stand - crafting at tier X, the portal chest, and
    travelling to any portal or sky anchor in ANY dimension for no crystals. It is the portal, carried: the thing
    the whole chain of portals has been leading to.
    Alternatives if you would rather: (a) the THIRD BLOCKHEAD - Portal X lets a world have three characters;
    (b) the WORLD SEED - plants a small private dimension of your own, a flat empty world to build in.

5. ENGINE WORK  (all of it as data fields the base game could use; no "if Dimensions is on")
================================================================================================================

  Dimensions      "dimensions" in worldgen.json: a name, a generator and its numbers, its biomes, veins, cave
                  decoration, creatures, sky, day length and light. A save holds one world per dimension, made
                  the first time it is entered. Save format goes up one; every older save still loads (as one
                  dimension).
  Generators      two new ones beside the overworld's: "islands" (the floating-island code the overworld already
                  has, made the whole world, with size and height bands and a cloud floor) and "caverns" (solid,
                  with halls carved by the cave code at ten times the scale, lakes and magma).
  Travel          a block that is a way to another dimension ("leads_to"), with the rule for where it may open
                  ("opens_above" / "opens_below"), and making the return one.
  Bench tiers     adding a tier to the end of a bench's chain from a mod (Q1), and recipes that ask for a named
                  tier or better.
  Hardness        blocks with a hardness, tools with a tier (Q6).
  New block rules buoyant (needs no support), falls (comes down when undermined), bounces, burns what touches it,
                  waters what is under it, charges (holds electricity when picked up), vents.
  New worn rules  glides, cooling (against heat), lights (a light you wear), hops further.
  Creatures       mounts (ridden and steered), climbers (walk on walls and roofs), burrowers, "only in the dark",
                  "only at night", bosses (size, health, an attack that calls something down).
  Timed boons     a drink or a food that gives boons for so many ticks.
  Vehicles off rails  the airship (flies), the drill rig (mines as it goes).
  Tests           one per rule, as always; a test for each dimension's generator; fixtures for the save format.

7. STAGES  (each ends tested, documented, committed and pushed; you play it and say what to change)
================================================================================================================

  1. THE ENGINE. Dimensions in a save, the two generators, travel, bench tiers from mods, hardness. A bare
     Aether and a bare Deep you can walk into: stone, soil, one ore each, the two portals and their trees.
  2. THE AETHER, MATERIAL. All its biomes, blocks, ores, gems, trees and plants; skysteel and moonsilver; the
     tool, armour, furnace and workbench tiers; Portal and Trade portal VII and VIII.
  3. THE AETHER, ALIVE. Its creatures, gliding, wind, lightning, thin air, floating and bouncing blocks, wind
     power, the new benches, food, building blocks, the airship, the Thunderbird.
  4. THE DEEP, MATERIAL. As stage 2, for the Deep; Portal and Trade portal IX and X; Workbench VI.
  5. THE DEEP, ALIVE. As stage 3: creatures, heat, dark, falling rock, gas, magma, geothermal power, the drill
     rig, taming, the fungal vat, fossils, the Crystal Golem.
  6. THE END. The Pocket Portal, sky anchors and recall stones; the recipe book and trade tiers for all of it;
     Discovery Road relic pools for both dimensions; balance; the README.

  Stage 1 is the risky one (it changes how a save is laid out and how the world is held in memory); the rest is
  mostly data, art and one rule at a time.
