Industrial Bench
5 Conveyor - 3 Heavy Bronze Ingot, 20s - Moves items at a pace of 1 item/tick
3 Gilded Conveyor - 3 Conveyor, 1 Royal Bronze Ingot, 20s - Moves items at a pace of 2 items/tick
Conveyor Extractor - 3 Royal Bronze Ingot, 20s - Place this adjacent to a block with an inventory to extract from the inventory to the conveyor. Allows you to filter to a specific item to be extracted.

Industrial Bench II - 2 Imperial Ingots
3 Imperial Conveyor - 3 Gilded Conveyor, 1 Imperial Ingot, 1m - Moves items at a pace of 5 items/ tick

Conveyors can move items into inventories that they are connected to. They prioritize the closest empty inventory. If there is no empty inventory, the conveyor does not accept input from extractors.

Industrial Furnace - Contains all standard electric furnace recipes but in half the time. This machine has a 1 slot input inventory and a 1 slot output inventory. (99 stack each). This machine can work automatically from the input inventory, but will not work if the output inventory is blocked by a different item than the output of the input inventory.
Factory - Has a 4 slot input inventory and a 1 slot output inventory. The factory does NOT accept conveyor input until it is configured. The factory works by attaching to any bench. The factory must be placed adjacent to a bench. The factory shows recipes of all benches it is adjacent to, and a recipe can be selected. Then, inventory slots for each of the required inputs are unlocked for conveyors to input. They ONLY accept as input the required items for the recipe. Recipes with more than 4 items cannot be crafted. They work on standard crafting time of the bench.
Factory II - Can be upgraded to a 6 slot input inventory and doubles crafting speed at the cost of 1 Processing Chip

Industrial Processor
Processing Chip - 3 Dreadsteel Ingots, 1 Imperial Ingot, 5m
Industrial Crate - 3 Dreadsteel Ingots, 1 Safe, 1 Imperial Ingot, 5m - 16 item inventory, accepts conveyor input, but you can filter up to 4 input items that it accepts.

Industrial Armory
Dreadsteel/Imperial armor at the same cost as all other armors but replacing the ingots, 3m per piece

Industrial Smithery 
Dreadsteel/Imperial Pickaxe/Axe/Machete, Dreadsteel Sword, and Imperial Longbow at the same cost as the other tools but replacing ingots. Imperial Longbow does 20 dmg, Dreadsteel Sword does 50 dmg.

Power Plant - Takes Dreadsteel and Imperial Ingots as fuel. Dreadsteel powers it for 1000 ticks, Imperial Ingots power it for 1800 Ticks. Produces 100 units of power per tick

All inputs into the alloy furnace are ingots
