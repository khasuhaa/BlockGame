MACHINATIONS OF MADNESS - WHAT WAS ASKED FOR, AND WHAT WAS BUILT

Asked for: redstone, in this game - torch, dust, repeaters, pistons, sticky pistons, slime blocks, redstone
blocks, pressure plates, levers, buttons - and more of my own choosing (moving walkways and the like); all of it
steampunk; another tier of the workbench; a great many machines for automatic factories, in the manner of
Minecraft's Create; immense freedom, deep mechanics, world-altering content.

Built (no plan was put to the owner first: the request said to work it out):

  Signals     Spark dust (wire, 15 squares), spark torch (inverter), spark block, repeater, delayer, and gate,
              latch, lever, button, pressure plate, weighing plate (items), slow and quick clocks, daylight sensor.
  Moved by    Piston and sticky piston (four ways, up to 24 blocks, carry whoever is in the way), resin block
  them        (slime), bore (digs), deployer (builds, sows, plants), spout and drain (water), blasting charge and
              great charge, signal hatch, signal lamp, gate belt, moving walkway and swift walkway.
  Steam       Boiler, windmill, steam pipe, pressure tank; belt, swift belt, mechanical arm, brass hopper; steam
              press, furnace, saw, loom, mill, smith; steam assembler; steam drill and the seeps it mines.
  Benches     Steam workbench (one more level of the workbench), gearworks, tinker's bench.
  94 blocks, 59 items, 63 recipes.

How it differs from redstone: dust joins on all four sides and runs through the air; a block is powered by a
signal beside it, never through a block; a torch reads what is under it; there is no comparator, observer or
hopper clock - a weighing plate, a clock and a latch do those jobs.

Left for later, if wanted: only the character being played is pushed, carried and weighed (creatures are, other
characters are not); nothing shows which dust is live; rotating shafts and gear ratios (Create's own idea) were
not attempted - steam is the game's electricity under another name; no fans, cranes, trains of blocks, or
contraptions that move as one.
