BEYOND THE SKY - WHAT WAS ASKED FOR, AND WHAT WAS BUILT

The proposal is ModDescriptions/Proposal2.txt. The owner asked for it built in one sitting, without the questions
being put first, and said:

  - Aim for video-game mechanics (not for realism).
  - NO ending: progression up to the red planet and through it. (So: no derelict, no last launch.)
  - Five more portal tiers: two for the Moon (VII, VIII), one for the asteroid belt (IX), two for the red planet
    (X, XI).
  - A LOT more exploration content in the places, and lots of content altogether.

So the proposal's questions were answered by that and by me:
  Q1 how real        Not at all. A rocket has FUEL and THRUST, added up from its parts; a way to a place asks for
                     so much of each; the cursor over the capsule says what there is against what is wanted.
  Q2 what a rocket is  Blocks on a pad, as proposed - but one column (with boosters standing either side), not
                     five wide, and it does not fly: at launch the stack is spent and whoever launched is there.
  Q3 air             As proposed, simply: a place with no air; a room is sealed if it is closed in by blocks and
                     has a wall behind every square of it; an air vent burning oxygen fills it. Outside one,
                     breath runs down as it does under water, and a suit is many times the breath.
  Q4 gravity         Two kinds only: none (orbit, the Belt: everybody floats, as in water) and low (the Moon, the
                     red planet: no fall is a fall). Nobody jumps higher.
  Q5 the places      Four, stacked under the world as Dimensions' are: orbit, the Moon, the Belt, the red planet.
  Q6 power           Its own (solar array, radioisotope generator, fission reactor, power cell, cable), on the
                     fields the base game already has.
  Q7 an ending       None, as told.

WHAT WAS BUILT (368 blocks, 644 items, 497 recipes, 46 creatures, 29 biomes, 4 places, 64 structures, 14 sets)

  Rocketry. A launch pad; five engines (and each "proven" on a test stand, which makes it surer); four tanks and
    their long kinds; an interstage; a launch escape tower (a failed launch then hurts nobody); a gantry to climb.
    Four capsules - orbital capsule, lunar lander, belt hauler, Mars lander - each made with the one before in
    it, each needing its chart from the observatory. An engine can fail: the rocket is lost and, with no tower,
    the pad is blown up and the crew hurt. The lander left at the far end is the way home, and asks for nothing.
  Knowledge. Charts are drawn at the observatory (the first from a sounding rocket's sample); surveys are made at
    the sample lab from samples of a place, and nothing of the next tier can be made until its survey has been.
    Eight precursor glyphs, found only in caskets, each unlock one piece of their technology.
  Air and bases. Hull, deck, viewports, habitat wall, dome glass, airlock doors, the air vent, an oxygen plant
    (electric), bunks, lights, consoles, lockers and about thirty other furnishings. Hydroponic trays and ten
    crops that grow only in air.
  Suits. Six, of four pieces, from the flight suit to the explorer exosuit; a thruster pack.
  Long makings. Each metal is refined in one to four stages before it is any use; electronics are five tiers,
    each built on a doped wafer of its place's crystal and on the tier before; every finished thing is built
    round a MODULE or an ASSEMBLY of its tier, each with the last tier's in it; and tier XI has the precursors'
    four-step making on top. Fewest makings to a finished thing: tier VI 4-5, VII 5-7, VIII 7-8, IX 8-9, X 10-12,
    XI 15.
  Portals and trade to XI, as asked: lunite block (VII), moon crystal (VIII), star sapphire (IX), martian steel
    block (X), Mars opal (XI); each new portal level makes one relic of its own.
  The places.
    ORBIT  Wreckage adrift: 12 kinds of thing to find (dead satellites, a station wreck, a lost lab, pods).
    THE MOON  A new "surface" generator: terraced ground under a black sky, craters, lava tubes whose floors are
      the country above; 8 kinds of country (mare, highlands, polar craters with ice, crystal fields, glass
      plains, a precursor site, rayed craters, lava plain); 16 structures (an old lander, a sealed outpost with
      a vent still in it, a precursor vault, a mass driver's ruin...).
    THE BELT  Asteroids of five kinds, a wreck field, a living garden, a precursor shell; 15 structures.
    THE RED PLANET  The same generator with a canyon sixty-four deep in it; 10 kinds of country (rust plains,
      dunes, the polar cap, canyon lands, a volcanic rise, salt flats, colony ruins, living ground, pyramid
      fields, a glass forest); 20 structures (colony domes with crops still in them, a crashed ship, pyramids,
      a sphinx, a great ring).
  To find: five kinds of cache; 12 mission logs; 16 relics in four sets, and each restorable into a better one;
    18 mineral specimens (a rock gives one now and then) and 8 fossils, each of which can be mounted; samples
    from the ground itself; 22 wild plants and four giant fungi; three great beasts (the Lunar colossus, the
    Belt leviathan, the Red wyrm) and a Pyramid guardian, each carrying a glyph.

WHAT WAS NOT BUILT, of the proposal
  - Rockets do not fly: there is no flight to watch, no stage falling back, nothing recovered. Only one column.
  - Engines do not get surer by flying; the test stand does that in one step.
  - No probes, no landing sites to map: a lander comes down wherever the engine puts it.
  - Satellites are things to set down and look at. They do nothing.
  - Weightlessness is floating, as in water: no pushing off walls, no thrusters needed, nothing drifts.
  - Air does not go bad, has no quantity, and does not leak slowly: a room has it or has not. No scrubbers,
    no hoses, no alarm.
  - Heat, cold and radiation do not kill; nights are not two weeks long.
  - No rovers to drive, no mining ship, no mass driver that works, no 3D printing of a base (the matter printer
    is a bench).
  - A station is built block by block; there are no modules to launch and dock.
  - The derelict and the ending were left out as told.
  - Nothing has been played. Every number - fuel, thrust, reliability, breath - is a first guess.
