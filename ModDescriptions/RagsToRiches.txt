RAGS TO RICHES - WHAT WAS ASKED FOR, AND WHAT WAS BUILT

Asked for: structures; people who live in villages; villages on a floating island, in a cave or on the ground;
trade portals that only work in a village and say so anywhere else; every village with prices of its own, many
things up to 20% dearer or cheaper, for buying and for selling (selling being 90% of buying); more mechanics of
my own choosing, perhaps things that can only be bought in certain villages; and houses that look good.

Built (no plan was put to the owner first: the request said to work it out):

  Villages      Three kinds - hamlet (ground, timber and thatch), delve (a hall under ground, stone and slate),
                aerie (an island of its own in the sky, whitewash and blue tile). At least one of each in every
                new world, and more in a wide one. A market in the middle with a bell, an awning and a trade
                portal (tier III, IV and VI); four to eight buildings either side: cottage, longhouse, house of
                two floors, tower, storehouse, well, garden.
  People        Villager, merchant, farmer, elder: they turn up on the village's floors and streets and wander.
                They do nothing else yet.
  Trade         Only in a village (within reach of its bell). Elsewhere the portal opens to a message.
  Prices        About six things in ten are up to 20% dearer or cheaper in any one village, and which, and by
                how much, changes every seven days. The menu shows the difference.
  Specialities  Fourteen things sold only in villages, four to a village; any other village buys them at half
                as much again.
  Other         Crates with loot in the houses; building blocks with recipes; sales paid in the biggest coins.

Left for later, if wanted: villagers who do something (buy, sell, ask for things, give work); reputation in a
village; a way to found a village of one's own; villages in the Aether and the Deep; roads between villages;
a ledger that remembers what each village charged.
