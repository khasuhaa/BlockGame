OBTAINMENT
Worked out from the data files (data/*.json) by a script, not by hand.

"Obtainable" means it can be got by playing, WITHOUT the Shop: dug or picked in a
generated world, left by a creature, found in a golden chest, brought by meditating,
given to a new character, or made from such things at a bench that can itself be got
and raised to the tier needed. The Shop sells every item for 1 copper coin, so counting
it would make everything obtainable.

Everything is treated as an item unless it can actually be put down in the world today
(the "X block" things, benches, and the blocks the game already had). Beds, doors,
columns, stairs, ladders and the like are items for now.

Totals: 370 items, 368 recipes, 30 kinds of bench.

1. UNOBTAINABLE ITEMS  (311 of 370)
==============================================================================
*** THE WORKBENCH ITSELF CANNOT BE MADE. ***
Its old recipe (1 dirt, at any portal) was removed with the rest and recipes.txt has no
line for it. Every bench is made at a Workbench, so as the data stands almost nothing
can be made at all. To keep this list useful it is split three ways: what has no source
(1a), what is still blocked even supposing a Workbench (1b), and what is held up by
nothing but the missing Workbench (1c).

*** THE KILN, THE FURNACE AND THE PRESS WAIT ON EACH OTHER. ***
Even with a Workbench, none of the three can be started:
    Kiln      needs 1 clay           - clay is only made at a Press tier 2
    Press II  needs 1 bronze ingot   - bronze is only made at a Furnace
    Furnace   needs 10 charcoal      - charcoal is only made at a Kiln
Nothing in the world gives clay, charcoal or bronze, so the loop has no way in. One
source for any one of them breaks it: clay to dig, say, or charcoal from the campfire.
With a Workbench alone 103 of the 370 items can be got; with a Workbench and a source of
clay, 208 can. Most of 1b is this loop and nothing else.

  - "Bucket of water" is a Tin bucket that is full (8/8). It is handed back empty, not used up.
  - Recipes that ask for a "tin bucket" itself (mixing bench, feeder chest, paint) use the bucket up.
  - "Bronze", "iron", "steel", "gold", "titanium" as amounts mean the ingots.
  - "Gold nuggets" are what gold ore blocks drop (the item that was called Gold ore).
  - "Coffee cherry" is what coffee trees bear (was Coffee bean). "Shop" is the trade portal.
  - "Flint/Stone/Gold spade" are the old shovels, renamed. "Basic torch" is the torch.
  - "Raw dodo meat" and "Raw fish" are the old dodo meat and fish.
  - Where a line gave no time, it takes the last time given above it in the same section.
  - "2 platinum stairs - platinum stairs" was read as: from 1 platinum block, like the rest.
  - Compost: each line is its own recipe, 5 of the thing for 1 compost.
  - Paint: 92 recipes, one per mix of 1, 2 or 3 of the 8 pigments; the paint carries the mixed colour.
  - The metalwork bench now has 2 tiers, as listed; its old third tier is gone.
  - Old recipes with no line in recipes.txt are gone, as asked. That includes the Workbench
    itself (was 1 dirt at any portal), the new player, the portal chest and the gem pickaxes.
