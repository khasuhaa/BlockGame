HOW THE DOCUMENT WAS READ
==============================================================================

  - "tin hat" (Clothing III) is read as the Tin foil hat; the Tin helmet is counted with the Tin
    Armor in Clothing IV.
  - "Rest of gem blocks in order" (Blocks VI) is read as the Sapphire, Emerald, Ruby and Diamond
    blocks, each with its column and stairs.
  - "Brick" is the item Red brick; "Lapis" is Lapis lazuli; "Worm" is Worms; "Flax Seed" is Flax
    seeds; "Gold ore" is the item called Gold nugget.
  - "Bucket of Milk" is the item Milk; "Cup of Coffee" is Coffee; "Coffee Cherry" is the item
    named coffee_bean, labelled Coffee cherry.
  - "door", "bed" and "wood gate" are the Wooden door, Wooden bed and Wooden gate; "oil lamp" is
    the Oil lantern; "Torch" is the Basic torch.
  - "gold chest" is the Golden chest; "feeeding chest" is the Feeder chest; "tinted window" is
    the Black window.
  - "elevator motor" is the Electric elevator motor; "resin" is Raw resin; "carbon fiber" is
    Carbon fibers.
  - "all pigments" is the eight pigments (Carbon black, Marble white, Red ochre, Indian yellow,
    Ultramarine blue, Tyrian purple, Copper blue, Emerald green). Paint and Paint stripper are
    not pigments and are in list A.
  - The toolsets are read as every tool of that material the game has: Flint (pickaxe, axe,
    spade, machete, spear); Stone (pickaxe, axe, spade); Bronze/Tin (bronze pickaxe, axe,
    machete and sword, tin spade); Iron (pickaxe, axe, machete, sword); Steel (pickaxe - the
    only steel tool); Gold/Titanium (gold pickaxe and spade, titanium pickaxe, axe and sword).
  - The Platinum block with its column and stairs, which the document left out, is in Blocks VI
    after titanium (the owner said Building VI; the document has no Building, and columns and
    stairs are with their blocks).
  - Everything in list A is in the category Special, which only the dev portal has.
  - What the upgrades cost is all in the game already: the five gems, Gold and Platinum coins,
    and crystals.
